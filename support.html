SUPPORT

How can we help?

Questions, bug reports and rules corrections all go to one place. We read every message.

Contact us

Email bristly.midair5v@icloud.com. It helps to include your device, the app version and data version (both in Settings), and a screenshot if something looks wrong.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Lodestar works offline with no account required. Your lists are stored on your device.

How do my lists sync between devices?

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, lists sync through iCloud when you’re signed in to the same Apple Account. Sync across platforms, including Android, is coming soon.

How do I share a list with a friend?

Open the list and use Share to send it as a file, as text or as a PDF.

A unit, card or rule looks wrong. What should I do?

Email us with the name of the unit, card or rule and what you expected to see. Include the data version from Settings so we can check it against the latest release.

Which devices does Lodestar support?

iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo and Mac, plus Android phones and tablets.
