Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works offline without one, and your lists stay on your device. An optional, free Lodestar account lets you sync lists between Apple devices and Android.

How do my lists sync between devices?

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, lists sync through iCloud when you’re signed in to the same Apple Account. To sync with Android too, sign in to a Lodestar account with your email, Apple or Google in Settings, Lodestar Account, then choose Lodestar Sync in Settings, List Sync on each device.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings, Lodestar Account, Delete Account. It deletes your account and every list synced to it, on every device, and can’t be undone. Lists in your device’s own library and in iCloud stay. If you can’t open the app, email us from the account’s address. More in the privacy policy.

How do I share a list with a friend?

Open the list and use Share to send it as a file, as text or as a PDF.

A unit, card or rule looks wrong. What should I do?

Email us with the name of the unit, card or rule and what you expected to see. Include the app version from Settings so we can check it against the latest release.

Which devices does Lodestar support?

iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo and Mac, plus Android phones and tablets.
