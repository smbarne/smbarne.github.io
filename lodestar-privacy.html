PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026. What Lodestar does with your data, in plain language.

Lodestar is an app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android by Stephen Barnes ("I", "me"). It is an unofficial companion for StarCraft: The Miniatures Game. This policy says what the app does with data.

The short version

  • You can use Lodestar without an account. Without one, your army lists stay on your device, or in your own iCloud if you use an Apple device.
  • If you create a Lodestar account, I store your email address and the lists you choose to sync, so they can reach your other devices.
  • The app sends anonymous usage events and crash reports so I can see what people use and fix bugs. They don’t include your name, email or the contents of your lists.
  • There are no ads, and nothing is sold or used to track you across other apps and websites.

Your lists

  • On your device. Lists are stored in the app’s private storage. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, lists that aren’t synced or exported are gone.
  • iCloud (iPhone, iPad and Mac). By default, the Apple apps sync your lists through your own iCloud account. That data goes to Apple, under Apple’s privacy policy. I can’t see it.
  • Android backup. If you turn on Android’s backup, Android may copy your lists and settings to your Google account. That is Google’s backup, not something the app sends to me.
  • Lodestar Sync. If you sign in and switch List Sync to Lodestar Sync, the app uploads your lists to my server so they sync across Apple and Android devices. See "Lodestar accounts" below.
  • Sharing. When you export or share a list, the app makes a file, a PDF or text and hands it to your device’s share sheet. The app you pick receives it under its own privacy policy. I don’t.

Lodestar accounts

An account is optional. You can create one with an email address and password, or sign in with Apple or Google.

What I store:

  • Your email address. With Sign in with Apple you can choose Hide My Email, and I only see the relay address Apple gives me.
  • An account ID that the server makes.
  • How you signed in (password, Apple or Google) and when. Your password is stored only as a hash. I never see it, and I never receive your Apple or Google password.
  • The lists you sync, with their names, factions, units and cards, and when they were changed.
  • The IP address and device details of each sign-in, kept briefly in the sign-in service’s security logs.

I use this to sign you in, to sync your lists, and to email you about your account, such as a confirmation link or a password reset. I don’t send marketing email.

The account service and database are run for me by Supabase (https://supabase.com), on servers in the United States. Account emails are sent through my email provider.

Signing in doesn’t change how your lists sync. Lists only go to my server after you choose Lodestar Sync.

Delete your account

You can delete your account inside the app:

  • iPhone, iPad and Mac: Settings, Lodestar Account, Delete Account.
  • Android: Settings, Lodestar Account, Delete Account.

Deleting your account removes your email address, your account ID and every list synced to it from my server, and signs you out on every device. It can’t be undone. Lists in your device’s own library and in iCloud aren’t affected. If I ever restore the server from a backup made before you deleted your account, I delete your data from it again before the server goes back into use.

If you can’t open the app, email bristly.midair5v@icloud.com from the address on the account and ask me to delete it. I will confirm when it’s done.

What the app sends without an account

Usage events (Mixpanel)

To see which parts of the app people use, the app sends events to Mixpanel (https://mixpanel.com):

  • Which section you opened (Rules, Factions, Lists or Settings).
  • That a list was created, duplicated, imported, deleted, exported or pinned, with the faction, the game size and the export format where they apply.
  • On iPhone, iPad and Android, Mixpanel’s automatic events: first open, app sessions and app updates.

Each event carries a random ID that Mixpanel makes the first time the app runs, plus the app version and device details such as the make and model, the operating system and its version, the screen size, the network type and the language. Mixpanel uses the IP address of the request to estimate your country and city, then discards it. Events don’t include list names or contents, what you type, your email or your account ID, so they aren’t linked to your account.

Crash and performance reports (Bugsnag)

When the app crashes, stops responding or hits an error it can’t recover from, such as a list that won’t open, it sends a report to Bugsnag (https://www.bugsnag.com). It also sends a session start when you open the app and measures how long the app takes to start and to show its screens.

A report can include the error and the code path that led to it, the app version, the device’s make, model, operating system, language, free memory and disk space, screen size and orientation, whether the device is rooted or jailbroken or an emulator, a short trail of recent events such as screen changes, and two random IDs that Bugsnag makes the first time the app runs. It doesn’t include your name, email, account ID, list names or contents, or what you type.

Both

Both services receive the data over an encrypted connection, process it for me as service providers and keep it for the period set by my plan with them. I use it only to improve and fix the app. There is no setting to turn it off. The random IDs are removed when you uninstall the app, and Android leaves them out of its backups.

What the app doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t show ads or use the advertising ID.
  • It doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and doesn’t sell or share your data with data brokers.
  • It doesn’t ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. On Android it uses only network access and the network state, to send the data above and to sync.

Children

Lodestar isn’t made for children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has created an account, email me and I will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to how it is used. Email bristly.midair5v@icloud.com and I will do what the law requires. You can delete your account and synced lists yourself at any time, as described above. The usage events and crash reports don’t carry your name or email, so I can’t find which ones are yours.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the effective date. A change to what the app collects will come with an app update.
