ANDROID BETA

Join the beta on Android

Three steps. Use the same Google account for each one: the account signed in to Google Play on your phone or tablet.

  1. Join the Lodestar Testers group

    Open the group and choose Join group. The group only controls access to the Google Play test. Other members can’t see your email address, and members can’t post to it.

    Join the group ↗

  2. Become a tester on Google Play

    Open the testing page and choose Become a tester.

    Join on Google Play ↗

  3. Install Lodestar

    On the same page, follow the link to download Lodestar from Google Play, or search for “Lodestar” in the Play Store app. Beta updates install like any other app update.

If something goes wrong

Google Play says the app isn’t available

Check that you joined the group with the same Google account you use in Google Play. Joining can take a few minutes to reach Google Play, so wait a little and open the testing page again. If it still doesn’t work, contact us.

I have more than one Google account on my phone

In the Play Store app, tap your profile picture and switch to the account you used to join the group before you open the testing page.

How do I leave the beta?

Open the testing page and choose Leave the program. You can also leave the group from its page.
